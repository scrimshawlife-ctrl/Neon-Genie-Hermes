Built for transitional builders

You already have an idea. What you need is a clear path through uncertainty — not more hype, and not a system that spends or ships for you.

Limited capital, real stakes

First cash, constrained launches, and honest “what can I do with what I have?” planning. No fictional resources.

Roadmap or approach — or both

Product coherence audits, opportunity mining, commercial models, and handoff packets when you’re ready for builders.

Using intelligence to reach the real world

Neon Genie researches public facts when they improve the decision, requests private facts instead of inventing them, and labels every material claim so you know what is observed, inferred, speculative, or not computable.

How a run moves

A practical sequence from stuck to sealed — always advisory. You stay in control of money, outreach, and code.

  1. 1.0

    Name the stuck point

    Who is blocked, what “done” looks like, and what you already know. Weak signals and half-formed ventures are valid starting points.

    You bring idea · constraints · audience · what you refuse to invent
  2. 2.0

    Find, request, refuse

    Public facts get researched when tools allow. Private facts become a DataRequest. Missing proof is labeled — never silently filled in.

    Conflict rule find public → request private → only then NOT_COMPUTABLE
  3. 3.0

    Shape the plan

    Opportunity and product packets, zero-option first-cash paths, commercial models, and Wayfinder-ready handoffs — with completion proof defined up front.

    Outputs packets · scorecards · data requests · run receipt
  4. 4.0

    Install and work in Hermes

    Load the skill, describe the job in plain language, and treat every recommendation as a draft. Neon Genie may not spend, publish, contact people, or mutate repositories.

    Install hermes skills install …/skills/neon-genie

Standards that keep you honest

Designed to be harder to impress and more decision-useful than idea chat — especially when capital and time are tight.

OBSERVED
Sourced fact you can point to
INFERRED
Reasoned from evidence, not free invention
SPECULATIVE
Hypothesis — useful, not proven
NOT_COMPUTABLE
Cannot be decided with available facts

Advisory only. Privacy defaults to local packaging runs; host and model retention stay outside this skill’s control. See the repository privacy contract for the full boundary.
