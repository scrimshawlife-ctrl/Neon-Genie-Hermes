## Required analysis
- target user and job;
- core mechanism;
- primary and secondary loops;
- feature interaction;
- information architecture;
- emotional and sensory pacing where applicable;
- technical and production burden;
- validation path;
- acceptance criteria;
- canon-versus-implementation drift.
