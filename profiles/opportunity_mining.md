# Opportunity Mining Profile

Find valuable state transitions that users cannot complete efficiently.

## Signal classes
- human friction;
- institutional friction;
- economic friction;
- technology frontier;
- repeated workarounds;
- exception patterns;
- unused rights, benefits, capacity, or capability.

## Runes
- `RUNE.NG.SENSE`
- `RUNE.NG.TRANSITION_MARKET`
- `RUNE.NG.INTERVENTION.SHAPE`
- `RUNE.NG.PORTFOLIO.ROUTE`

## Required fields
affected user, economic buyer, authority holders, transition market, coordination gap, service-first wedge, distribution, completion proof, and failure modes.
